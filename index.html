The weekend

Round Table is better experienced than explained.

00 Aberdeen 2027 is an international Round Table gathering: a chance to meet people from across the world, spend time together, and see our city through the eyes of its hosts. There will be planned activities and big nights, but also the unplanned moments that become the best stories.

Come for the weekend. Leave with friends in places you have never been.

Our history

One hundred years of getting together and doing something useful.

Round Table began in Norwich in 1927 with a simple idea: young people gathering around a table to exchange ideas, learn from one another, and play a part in the life of their communities. The Aberdeen Table brings that same spirit to 00 Aberdeen 2027: open, social, practical, and ready to show its guests a very good time.
