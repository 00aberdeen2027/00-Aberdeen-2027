About the event

An annual international meetup for connection, culture, and discovery.

00 Aberdeen 2027 is designed for people who value meaningful connection. It brings together global attendees from business, culture, public life, and innovation to meet new people, explore fresh perspectives, and enjoy the energy of visiting new places together. This is a gathering centered on relationships as much as ideas.

Our history

Rooted in service since 1937.

The organiser of this year’s edition is Round Table 100 – Aberdeen, a club with a proud heritage dating back to 1937. As the oldest active Scottish table, it brings a legacy of fellowship, community leadership, and local impact to every gathering.
